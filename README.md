# ⚡ Smart Energy Saving Reminder System

### 🌱 Smart Technology for a Greener and Energy-Efficient Future

The **Smart Energy Saving Reminder System** is a web-based application designed to promote energy conservation and help users reduce unnecessary electricity consumption. It encourages smart energy habits by providing useful reminders, electricity usage calculations, and practical energy-saving tips.

The main goal of this project is to make people aware of their electricity consumption, reduce electricity bills, and contribute towards a more sustainable environment.

## 🚀 Features

- ⚡ **Energy Calculator:** Helps users estimate their electricity consumption based on appliance usage.
- 🔔 **Smart Reminders:** Provides reminders to switch off unused electrical appliances and avoid energy wastage.
- 💡 **Electricity Bill Calculator:** Estimates electricity bills based on energy consumption and applicable rates.
- 🌿 **Energy-Saving Tips:** Offers practical suggestions to reduce electricity usage.
- 📊 **Interactive Dashboard:** Displays useful energy-related information in an attractive dashboard.
- 📱 **Responsive Design:** Provides a user-friendly experience across desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5** – For structuring the website.
- **CSS3** – For styling, layouts, animations, and responsive design.
- **JavaScript** – For calculations, interactive features, and application functionality.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

1. Promote awareness about electricity conservation.
2. Reduce unnecessary energy consumption.
3. Help users monitor and estimate electricity usage.
4. Encourage environmentally responsible habits.
5. Support a greener and more sustainable future.

## 🌍 Environmental Impact

Saving electricity helps reduce energy demand and can lower greenhouse gas emissions, particularly when electricity is generated from fossil fuels. By encouraging responsible electricity usage, this system supports environmental protection and sustainable living.
